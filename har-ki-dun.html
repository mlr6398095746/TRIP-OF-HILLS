7-Day Trek Itinerary

Day 1: Dehradun to Sankri

Distance: 210 km

Driving Time: Approximately 9–10 hours

Begin your journey from Dehradun and drive towards Sankri, the gateway to the Har Ki Dun Trek. Enjoy beautiful mountain landscapes and scenic views along the way.

Overnight Stay: Sankri

Day 2: Sankri → Gangad → Osla

Drive: 22 km

Trek: 4 km

Trek Time: Approximately 4–5 hours

After breakfast, drive from Sankri to Gangad. From Gangad, begin your trek towards the beautiful Himalayan village of Osla. Enjoy scenic trails, forests and mountain views.

Overnight Stay: Osla

Day 3: Osla → Har Ki Dun Valley

Distance: 10–12 km

Trek Time: Approximately 5–6 hours

Start your trek towards the spectacular Har Ki Dun Valley. Walk through beautiful forests, meadows and Himalayan landscapes. Reach Har Ki Dun and enjoy the magnificent surroundings.

Overnight Stay: Har Ki Dun

Day 4: Explore Har Ki Dun Valley & Marinda Tal

Spend the day exploring the breathtaking Har Ki Dun Valley. Enjoy spectacular views of Swargarohini and other snow-covered Himalayan peaks.

Take a short trek towards Marinda Tal and explore the beautiful surroundings before returning to the Har Ki Dun campsite.

Overnight Stay: Har Ki Dun

Day 5: Har Ki Dun → Osla

Distance: 10–12 km

After breakfast, begin your return trek from Har Ki Dun Valley towards Osla. Enjoy the beautiful Himalayan trail and mountain views along the way.

Overnight Stay: Osla

Day 6: Osla → Gangad → Sankri

Trek: 4 km

Drive: 22 km

Trek from Osla to Gangad. From Gangad, board the vehicle and drive back to Sankri. Relax after your Himalayan adventure.

Overnight Stay: Sankri

Day 7: Sankri → Dehradun

Distance: 210 km

Driving Time: Approximately 9–10 hours

After breakfast, start your return journey from Sankri to Dehradun. Enjoy the scenic mountain roads and beautiful Himalayan landscapes.

Trip Ends: Dehradun

Difficulty Level

Moderate

The Har Ki Dun Trek is suitable for trekkers with a reasonable level of fitness. Regular walking, jogging or cardio exercises before the trek can help you enjoy the trek comfortably.

Best Time to Visit

April – June

Pleasant weather, beautiful landscapes and clear Himalayan mountain views.

September – December

Crisp mountain weather, beautiful scenery and spectacular Himalayan views.
