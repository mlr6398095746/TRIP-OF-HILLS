5-Day Trek Itinerary

Choose Quick for a simple overview or Detailed for the full day-by-day plan.

01

Dehradun → Hanuman Chatti

🚗 180 km • 6–7 hrs

Drive from Dehradun to Hanuman Chatti. Reach the base area, settle in and prepare for the trek.

02

Hanuman Chatti → Kandola Chhani

🥾 6 km • 6–7 hrs

Trek through the mountain trail towards Kandola Chhani, enjoying the forest and Himalayan surroundings.

03

Kandola Chhani → Seema Thatch

🥾 Approx. 5–6 km • 5–6 hrs

Continue towards Seema Thatch through beautiful forest sections and open meadow landscapes.

04

Seema Thatch → Gulabi Kantha Summit → Kandola Chhani

🏔️ Summit Day

Early start for the Gulabi Kantha summit, enjoy the Himalayan views and descend to Kandola Chhani.

05

Kandola Chhani → Hanuman Chatti → Dehradun

🥾 Trek + 🚗 Drive

Descend to Hanuman Chatti and drive back to Dehradun, completing your Gulabi Kantha adventure.

01

Drive from Dehradun to Hanuman Chatti

🚗 Drive Distance: 180 km • 6–7 hrs

Start from Dehradun and travel towards Hanuman Chatti through scenic mountain roads. After arrival, check in, relax and prepare for the trek.

02

Trek from Hanuman Chatti to Kandola Chhani

🥾 Trek Distance: 6 km • 6–7 hrs

After breakfast, begin the trek towards Kandola Chhani. The trail takes you through beautiful mountain surroundings and forest sections. Overnight stay at camp.

03

Trek from Kandola Chhani to Seema Thatch

🥾 Approx. 5–6 km • 5–6 hrs

Continue uphill towards Seema Thatch. As the trail opens up, enjoy wider views and peaceful alpine meadow scenery. Overnight stay at camp.

04

Seema Thatch → Gulabi Kantha Summit → Kandola Chhani

🏔️ Main Summit Day

Start early for the Gulabi Kantha summit. Take time to enjoy the mountain panorama and then descend back towards Kandola Chhani for the night.

05

Kandola Chhani → Hanuman Chatti → Dehradun

🥾 Trek + 🚗 Drive

After breakfast, trek down to Hanuman Chatti. From there, drive back to Dehradun. Your Trip Of Hills Gulabi Kantha trek ends with beautiful mountain memories.
