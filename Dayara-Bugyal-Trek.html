🌿 Dayara Bugyal — A Himalayan Meadow Adventure

The Dayara Bugyal Trek takes you from the village landscapes around Natin into beautiful high-altitude meadows and Himalayan terrain. The route combines scenic forests, open grasslands, mountain views and seasonal snow.

Your journey includes the climb towards Gui and Upper Chilapada, the summit day to Dayara Top, and the descent towards Nayata before returning to Natin and driving back to Dehradun.

🌲 Forest Trails 🌿 Alpine Meadows ❄️ Snow Experience 🏔️ Himalayan Views 🐎 Mountain Landscapes 📸 Photo Moments
Day-by-Day Plan

5 Days of Himalayan Adventure

Follow the complete Dayara Bugyal Trek itinerary below.

DAY 1

🚐 Dehradun → Natin Village

Drive from Dehradun to Natin Village, the base point for the trek.

Altitude2,250 m / 7,400 ft
Distance180 km
Duration7–8 hrs drive
🏠 Accommodation: Guesthouse • 🧳 Cloakroom facility available at Natin Village base camp.
DAY 2

🥾 Natin Village → Gui

Begin trekking from Natin Village towards the scenic settlement and meadows around Gui.

Start2,250 m / 7,400 ft
Finish3,000 m / 9,800 ft
Trek4–5 km • 4–5 hrs
DAY 3

🌲 Gui → Upper Chilapada

Continue deeper into the high-altitude landscape as the trail climbs towards Upper Chilapada.

Start3,000 m / 9,800 ft
Finish3,400 m / 10,800 ft
Trek3.5 km • 2–2.5 hrs
DAY 4

🏔️ Upper Chilapada → Dayara Top → Nayata

The highlight day: trek towards Dayara Top and then descend to Nayata through spectacular Himalayan scenery.

Upper Chilapada3,400 m / 10,800 ft
Dayara Top3,700 m / 12,000 ft
Nayata2,800 m / 9,200 ft
🥾 Upper Chilapada → Dayara Top: 4.5 km • 5–6 hrs   |   Dayara Top → Nayata: 6 km • 3–4 hrs
DAY 5

🥾 Nayata → Natin → Dehradun

Complete the final trek to Natin Village and then drive back to Dehradun.

Nayata2,800 m / 9,200 ft
Natin2,250 m / 7,400 ft
Trek6 km • 3–3.5 hrs
🚐 Drive back to Dehradun: 180 km • approximately 7–8 hrs.
