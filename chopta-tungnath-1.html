🗓️ Trek Itinerary

✨ 5 DAYS HIMALAYAN ADVENTURE ✨

🚗 Day 1 – Rishikesh to Sari Village

Begin your Himalayan journey with a scenic drive from Rishikesh to the beautiful Sari Village.

📍 Route: Rishikesh → Sari Village

🚗 Drive Distance: 190 km

⏱️ Travel Time: 7–8 hours

🌿 Day 2 – Sari Village to Deoriatal

Trek through beautiful mountain trails to reach peaceful Deoriatal Lake, surrounded by magnificent Himalayan scenery.

🥾 Trek Distance: 3 km

⏱️ Trek Duration: 1–2 hours

🌲 Enjoy peaceful forest trails and beautiful mountain views.
🏕️ Day 3 – Deoriatal to Tala Village & Baniyakund

Trek from Deoriatal towards Tala Village and continue by road to Baniyakund.

🥾 Trek Distance: 10 km

⏱️ Trek Duration: 6–7 hours

🚗 Drive Distance: 15 km

⏱️ Drive Duration: 45 minutes

🛖 Accommodation: Cottages / Swiss Camp

🏔️ Enjoy beautiful Himalayan landscapes and relax at your mountain accommodation.
⛰️ Day 4 – Chopta, Tungnath & Chandrashila Trek

Drive from Baniyakund to Chopta, then trek towards Tungnath Temple and Chandrashila summit. Return to Baniyakund after the trek.

🥾 Trek Distance: 9–10 km, both ways

⏱️ Trek Duration: 7–8 hours

🚗 Drive Distance: 5 km one way

⏱️ Drive Duration: 15 minutes one way

📈 Difficulty Level: Moderate

🍲 Meals: Hot lunch

🌲 Landscapes & Trail:
Dense forests • Steep uphill climb • Zigzag trails • Alpine meadows • Scenic ridge walks
🏡 Day 5 – Baniyakund to Rishikesh

Enjoy the final views of the Himalayas before driving back to Rishikesh, carrying wonderful memories of your trek.

📍 Route: Baniyakund → Rishikesh

🚗 Drive Distance: 190 km

⏱️ Travel Time: 7–8 hours
