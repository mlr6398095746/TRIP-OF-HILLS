🥾 Kedarkantha Trek Itinerary

01

Dehradun to Sankri Drive

🚗 Distance/Time: Approximately 200 km • 9–10 hours

After breakfast, begin your scenic journey from Dehradun towards Sankri. Enjoy beautiful mountain valleys, forests and traditional Himalayan villages along the route.

On arrival at Sankri, check in at your accommodation, relax and prepare for the trek. Your trek leader will provide a briefing about the upcoming adventure.

🏕️ Overnight Stay: Sankri
02

Sankri to Juda Ka Talab

🥾 Distance/Time: Approximately 4 km • 4–5 hours

After breakfast, start your trek from Sankri towards the beautiful Juda Ka Talab. The trail passes through dense pine and oak forests, small streams and scenic mountain clearings.

Juda Ka Talab is one of the most beautiful highlights of the Kedarkantha trail.

🏕️ Overnight Stay: Juda Ka Talab Camp
03

Juda Ka Talab to Kedarkantha Base Camp

🥾 Distance/Time: Approximately 4 km • 3–4 hours

Continue your trek towards Kedarkantha Base Camp through beautiful forests and open meadows. The trail gradually climbs and offers stunning views of the surrounding Himalayan peaks.

On reaching the base camp, enjoy the magnificent mountain scenery and prepare yourself for the summit climb.

🏕️ Overnight Stay: Kedarkantha Base Camp
04

🏆 Kedarkantha Summit – 12,500 ft

🥾 Distance/Time: Approximately 7–9 km • 6–8 hours

The most exciting day of your adventure begins early in the morning. Start the summit climb from Kedarkantha Base Camp and make your way towards the magnificent Kedarkantha Peak.

Once at the summit, enjoy spectacular panoramic views of the Himalayan ranges and surrounding snow-covered peaks.

After spending memorable moments at the summit, begin your descent towards the lower campsite.

🏕️ Overnight Stay: Camp
05

Camp to Sankri Trek

🥾 Distance/Time: Approximately 4–6 km • 3–5 hours

After breakfast, begin your descent towards Sankri. Walk through beautiful Himalayan forest trails and enjoy the final views of the mountains.

On reaching Sankri, relax at your accommodation and celebrate the completion of your Kedarkantha summit adventure.

🏕️ Overnight Stay: Sankri
06

Sankri to Dehradun Drive

🚗 Distance/Time: Approximately 200 km • 9–10 hours

After breakfast, start your return journey from Sankri to Dehradun. Enjoy the scenic mountain roads and beautiful valleys along the way.

Your unforgettable Kedarkantha Trek adventure with Trip Of Hills comes to an end as you reach Dehradun.

🏁 Trip Ends: Dehradun
